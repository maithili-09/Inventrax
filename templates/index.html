ABOUT INVENTRAX

A smarter way to manage everyday inventory.

Designed for businesses that need clear stock visibility, reliable records and useful reports without unnecessary complexity.

Inventrax provides a centralized platform where businesses can manage products, monitor stock quantities, maintain supplier information and record purchase orders and sales efficiently.

The system also helps businesses identify low-stock products and keep inventory information updated when products are purchased or sold. This makes everyday stock management more organized and easier to monitor.

With date-based analytical reports, users can review relevant inventory activities for a selected period and understand their business operations more effectively.
